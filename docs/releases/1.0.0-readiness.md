# `1.0.0` Launch Readiness Gate

This document records the evidence for the 1.0.0 launch: the soak of the
release candidate in production (Phase 5 Step 19; ADR-0013 decision 18), then
the launch readiness gate (Phase 5 Step 20), whose GO or NO-GO the maintainer
records here. Step 19 created it with its first two sections; Step 20
evaluated them on 2026-11-27 and added sections 3 to 17.

The candidate is `1.0.0rc2`. `1.0.0rc1`'s soak ended early, on the
maintainer's decision of 2026-11-23, to take a High finding's fix into the
release (section 1.0); rc1's record stays below as evidence.

---

  ("deploy and verify the API") finished in run 45312046292. Its `site` job

  were not waited out: rc2's soak replaces them.
