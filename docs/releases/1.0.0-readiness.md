# `1.0.0` Launch Readiness Gate

This document records the evidence for the 1.0.0 launch: the soak of the
release candidate in production (Phase 5 Step 19; ADR-0013 decision 18), then
the launch readiness gate (Phase 5 Step 20), whose GO or NO-GO the maintainer
records here. Step 19 created it with its first two sections; Step 20
evaluates them and adds the rest.

The candidate is `1.0.0rc2`. `1.0.0rc1`'s soak ended early, on the
maintainer's decision of 2026-11-23, to take a High finding's fix into the
release (section 1.0); rc1's record stays below as evidence.

---

## 1. The soak

- **Candidate:** `1.0.0rc2`, tag `v1.0.0rc2` (section 2.2).
- **Start:** 2026-11-24 02:39:25 UTC, when `deploy-production.yml`'s `api` job
  ("deploy and verify the API") finished in run 45312046292. Its `site` job
  finished at 02:41:34 UTC.
- **End:** 2026-11-27 02:39:25 UTC, three days after the start: the length the maintainer set on
  2026-11-23 under Step 20's NO-GO clause ("the next candidate is `1.0.0rc2`
  with a soak the maintainer sets"). ADR-0013 decision 18's seven days were
  rc1's.
- **Monitors:** the status page's monitors and `production-health.yml` run
  through Cloudflare Access with production's service token for the whole soak;
  nothing about them changes for it.

### 1.0 The first candidate's soak, ended early

- **Candidate:** `1.0.0rc1`, tag `v1.0.0rc1` (section 2.1).
- **Start:** 2026-11-20 02:23:36 UTC, when `deploy-production.yml`'s `api` job
  ("deploy and verify the API") finished in run 44658532719. Its `site` job
  finished at 02:26:02 UTC.
- **Planned end:** 2026-11-27 02:23:36 UTC, seven days after the start.
- **Ended:** 2026-11-23 22:09 UTC, by the maintainer's decision. On
  2026-11-23 the maintainer found on production that every select's open list
  was near-white on white in the dark theme in Chrome on Windows: the options
  were transparent, and the browser draws a transparent list over white. That
  is a High finding (`docs/design/dogfooding.md` §3: "a page looks broken"),
  which section 7's design and UX criterion cannot pass with open. Offered a
  waiver or a second candidate, the maintainer chose to fix it before launch.
  rc1's remaining days could not change which candidate launches, so they
  were not waited out: rc2's soak replaces them.
- **What rc2 adds:** #388 (the select lists' colours, with a check that reads
  every select's options in both themes) and #387 (the calculator's and the
  explorer's message for a request Access redirects or the network refuses,
  found the same day: "the run was not sent" when the run had been
  redirected). Nothing else: the pull requests held in section 1.2 stay held.
