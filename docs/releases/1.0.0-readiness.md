# `1.0.0` Launch Readiness Gate

This document records the evidence for the 1.0.0 launch: the soak of the
release candidate `1.0.0rc1` in production (Phase 5 Step 19; ADR-0013 decision
18), then the launch readiness gate (Phase 5 Step 20), whose GO or NO-GO the
maintainer records here. Step 19 created it with its first two sections; Step
20 evaluates them and adds the rest.

---

## 1. The soak

- **Candidate:** `1.0.0rc1`, tag `v1.0.0rc1` (section 2).
- **Start:** to be recorded at production's deploy of `v1.0.0rc1`: the time
  `deploy-production.yml`'s `api` job finished, in UTC, with its run.
- **End:** seven days after the start.
- **Monitors:** the status page's monitors and `production-health.yml` run
  through Cloudflare Access with production's service token for the whole soak;
  nothing about them changes for it.

### 1.2 What may merge during the soak

The candidate soaked is the candidate released: 1.0.0's archives differ from
`1.0.0rc1`'s only in version metadata (ADR-0013 decision 18). So until Step 21:

- **Merges:** Dependabot security updates and `hotfix/` branches, each a
  security fix.
- **Records:** a pull request that changes only files no part of the candidate
  ships (`docs/releases/`, `docs/roadmap/` and the in-browser examples' pin under
  `docs/wasm/`): neither distribution carries them (the sdist holds `src/`,
  `README.md`, `pyproject.toml`, `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`), the site and the
  image are built without them, and the documentation build leaves out
  `releases/` and `roadmap/`.
- **Held until Step 21:** everything else, including every Dependabot version
  update. Held at the start: #103, #187, #246, #247, #250, #251, #340, #345,
  #353 and #356.
